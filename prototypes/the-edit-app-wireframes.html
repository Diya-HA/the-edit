1The Edit — full app map (low-fi)

Pinterest-style shopping app built around named aesthetics. The core loop: describe a vibe (free-text to the AI or pick a preset) → the app curates one cohesive on-aesthetic feed across brands → save, board, and buy. Four tabs: Feed · Search · Boards · Settings. Handwritten notes call out where the USP lives.
