1The Edit: one committed direction

Built from the wireframe with the full design-system treatment: pure white canvas, vermillion accent, Impressionist tints and the pointillist texture on every painted surface. The look deck now lives under Search → Looks, save gestures live on every heart (tap to save, hold to pick a board), and full-bleed browsing is a toggle at the top of home. Every product image is a real placeholder. Drag a photo onto it and it stays. All the microcopy is rewritten to sound like a kind, human stylist. 1a opens on the welcome flow and clicks through into the whole app; accent pigment and tone palette are in Tweaks.
